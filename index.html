Watching

The lab injects fuel automatically at 6° before top dead centre. Start the timing challenge to fire the injector yourself.

Simplified model: 15.6:1 compression (as in the Hilux 2.8), no turbo boost, and the fuel lights only if the air is above about 210 °C when it's sprayed in. Real engines also have an ignition delay and several injections per cycle.
